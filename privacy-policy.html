Privacy Policy

App: My Money
Last updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how My Money (“the App”) handles information. My Money is a personal finance organizer. It is designed to keep most of your data on your own device.

1. Who we are

My Money is published by the developer of this app. Contact: dahbi.abderazak121@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

By default, the App stores information locally on your device, including:

  • Income, expenses, budgets, wallets, and balances you enter
  • Recurring bills, purchase timers, tips, and reminders
  • App settings such as language, currency, and theme
  • Optional PIN lock data (stored as a secure hash on your device)

The App does not require an account to use core features. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Optional Google / Gmail sync

If you choose to connect Google for sync or verification, the App may access:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Google Drive app data storage for backup/sync files
  • Gmail send permission only when needed to send a verification code to yourself

This happens only after you provide your own Google OAuth Client ID (when required) and grant permission. You can disconnect Google at any time from inside the App.

4. Backups and file export

You may export or import a plan/backup file, copy plan data, or send a backup via email apps on your device. Those files are controlled by you. Keep them private.

5. How we use information

Information you enter is used only to:

  • Provide budgeting, tracking, reminders, and related app features
  • Save your preferences on your device
  • Perform optional sync/backup when you enable it

6. Data storage and security

Core financial data is stored locally (for example in browser/device storage). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please keep backups and protect your device.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Third-party services

If you enable Google features, Google’s own terms and privacy policy also apply to that service. The App may use standard web technologies (such as a service worker) for offline use.

9. Your choices

  • Use the App offline without connecting Google
  • Export/delete your local data using in-app options (including reset)
  • Disconnect Google sync
  • Uninstall the App to remove local app data from that device

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

11. Contact
