Data Deletion Request

App: My Money
Developer contact: dahbi.abderazak121@gmail.com
Last updated: August 20, 2026

This page explains how users of My Money can delete their data or request deletion assistance.

1. Delete data yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the My Money app.
  2. Go to the home settings area.
  3. Use Reset All Data to erase local financial data stored on your device.
  4. If Google sync is connected, tap Disconnect / remove the linked Gmail connection.
  5. If you saved backup/plan files on your device or Drive, delete those files manually.
  6. Optionally uninstall the app to remove remaining local app data from that device.

2. Request deletion by email

If you need help, email: dahbi.abderazak121@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Subject: My Money Data Deletion Request
  • The Gmail address you used for optional sync (if any)
  • The device/platform you used
  • Confirmation that you want your My Money related data deleted

We aim to respond within 30 days.

3. What data is deleted

  • Local app data you entered (transactions, budgets, timers, settings, PIN hash) when you reset/uninstall
  • Optional sync connection metadata on the device when you disconnect
  • Any developer-accessible sync-related records tied to your request, if applicable

4. What may be retained

  • Backup/plan files that remain in your own Google Drive or email inbox until you delete them
  • Limited records required for legal, security, or abuse-prevention reasons, if any, for a limited period
